Stop paying top-dollar LLM tokens for simple routing decisions- using a "System One"-Decision Layer with RLCDIn modern enterprise AI architectures, up to 40% of standard LLM requests do not require generative, token-by-token reasoning. Instead, they require fast, probabilistic decision-making (e.g., intent routing, compliance gating, schema classification, or model orchestration). Relying on heavy, autoregressive System Two LLMs (like GPT-4, Claude, or LLaMA) for these tasks leads to unnecessary latency (1–3 seconds), high inference costs, and non-deterministic text outputs that require complex [parsing.By](https://www.linkedin.com/safety/go/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fparsing%2EBy&urlhash=21bl&mt=egjOYDKgw9rWP2tN8SlmK2Zj0uNcXc0YOTcxjGKTHbhqJa8hxpQumPerclaAGnNs_NbttrCs6IGL4x7xhZva14I5fFSQGD2GoHdATHwYffvL9NKOalMud6Ey&isSdui=true) introducing a dedicated System One decision engine—specifically leveraging TypeSafe AIs Jev model trained via Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions (RLCD)—enterprises can route simple classification and evaluation calls before hitting heavy generative models.The Problem: Over-Provisioning System Two ModelsCurrent enterprise LLM pipelines often route every step of a workflow through generative models: Paying standard per-token generation pricing for discrete classification or routing logic.Challenges other than Pricing : i. Latency Bottlenecks: Generative models stream text sequentially, taking 500ms–2000ms just to return simple binary or categorical outputs.Poor Calibration: ii. Traditional RLHF-trained models are optimized to sound persuasive, often returning overconfident answers without reliable probability scores for decision confidence.The Architecture Solution: Two-Tier "System One / System Two" RoutingBy placing a low-latency decision model like Jev at the enterprise entry tier, we evaluate incoming states in parallel using Type-Safe Primitives (Choice, Score, Noul) backed by mathematically calibrated confidence scores (Brier score / binning calibration).Key Technical Primitives:Instead of unparsed JSON or raw text tokens, Jev evaluates state against strict typed questions in a single parallel pass.1>Choice: Evaluates categorical classification across up to 255 predefined choices.2>Score: Assigns bounded numerical ratings or ordered level scores.3>Noul: Produces exact probabilities for binary/boolean decisions to establish clear execution thresholds.Impact Metrics amp; ROI Projection:1>Cost Reduction: 30%–40% net reduction in monthly enterprise LLM spend by offloading simple parsing and routing calls away from expensive generative endpoints.2>Latency Optimization: Decision-layer evaluation latency drops from 1,500ms to lt;100ms (up to 15x faster), improving total end-to-end user experience.3>Workflow Safety: Calibrated confidence scores allow deterministic thresholding (e.g., auto-executing actions only when Jev confidence $gt; 0.90$, otherwise routing to human review or a fallback LLM).
